WE ARE CHANGING THE WAY HARDWARE is developed FOR LAUNCH VEHICLES

 

While working for our clients to select hardware for their systems, we became frustrated with the current state of Quick Disconnects on the market. We found no aerospace-grade, off-the-shelf, fittings that were readily available and designed with New Space in mind. Instead, we found custom design projects, with 26 week lead times, and flight-side weights better suited to a heavy-lift vehicle.

So we jumped.

OUR LINE OF QUICK DISCONNECTS IS FLIGHT PROVEN.

Launch Co. has built quick disconnect systems for fluids ranging from hydrocarbons, to oxidizers, to sub-cooled cryogenics, to pyrophorics; operating pressure ranges up to 10,000 psi, redundant sealing systems, and ASME B31.1 compliance. These fittings are perfect for launch vehicles, spacecraft, test stands, launch sites, or any other place where fluids systems need to be simply & reliably mated.

Our line of quick disconnects spans 1/2" nominal up to 4" nominal, though other sizes are available on request. We offer slip fittings, dual-side checking fittings, and single-side checking fittings on either the flight or ground side. Both cryogenic & standard operating temperatures are available, and lines up to 10,000 psi operating pressure.

We have full cut sheets, analysis & test data available for review. Contact us to discuss your need & how our QD's can help streamline your fluids interfaces.

Hardware

Ground-to-Vehicle Quick Disconnect (QD) Fittings 

  • TLC developed QD fittings from the ground up for fast, assured sealing, and sealing at T=0. 

  • These fittings are meant to be the connection between the vehicle and the ground systems. 

  • We offer standard "full port" line sizes from 1/4" up to 2" in the following configurations: o Slip fittings - No internal poppet checking mechanisms. 

    • Single checking fittings - Automatic checking mechanism on only one side (either ground or vehicle). 

    • Double checking fittings - Internal automatic checking mechanism on the ground side and on the flight side that snap shut after disconnecting. 

Orbital Re-Fueling Fittings 

  • Orbital Re-Fueling Fittings are used for remote fluid resupply operations between space vehicles. 

    • Our orbital fittings are modularly designed to be compatible with our QD fitting line. QD flight-side fittings will mate to our Orbital Fittings helping to drive standards from launch site to orbit. 

    • The vehicle (male) side has a mechanical "latch" to the supply (female) side that anchors the fitting during fluids transfer. This latching feature allows the fitting to reaction all pressurization forces, saving mass and complexity on mating plates. 

    • We offer "full port" line sizes from 1/4" up to 2" in double-checking configuration.
