# About

Oscar H. is a graduate student in the first year of the [Master of Science in Computer Science](https://www.ics.hawaii.edu/academics/graduate-programs/ms "Master of Science in Computer Science") degree program at the [University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa](https://www2.hawaii.edu/~oih "University of Hawaii at Manoa"). 

## Bio
Oscar obtained a _Master of Science_ in mathematics from the [University of Alaska Fairbanks](https://sites.google.com/alaska.edu/oihernandez "oihernandez@alaska.edu") and a _Bachelor of Arts_ in computer science from [Bard College at Simon's Rock](https://simons-rock.edu/news/students-win-prize-physics-research.php "Simon's Rock") after first obtaining the _Associate of Arts_ in liberal arts.

His experience lies primarily in computer science, engineering, and the mathematical sciences.
