# About

Oscar solves hard problems by building automated hardware/software systems and integrating with any technology required. He is passionate about working with nonprofits, academic researchers, and especially startups.

## Bio
As an undergraduate, he studied liberal arts and graduated from Bard College at Simon's Rock with a _Bachelor of Arts_ in mathematics and computer science in the spring of 2017. Since then, he's gained valuable experience freelancing his services as a full-stack software engineer and system administrator. He continues to learn [science](/science "formal, natural, and applied sciences"), and is now especially interested in embarking on projects with mechanical and electrical components.

For previous projects, see [Portfolio](/portfolio "Portfolio").

Oscar spends most of his time dealing with robotics software and websites.
