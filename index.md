# About

Oscar Hernandez just graduated from the [Master of Science in Computer Science](https://www.ics.hawaii.edu/academics/graduate-programs/ms "Master of Science in Computer Science") degree program at the [University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa](https://www2.hawaii.edu/~oih "University of Hawaii at Manoa"). 

## Bio
After obtaining the _Associate in Arts_ in liberal arts from [Bard College at Simon's Rock](https://simons-rock.edu/news/students-win-prize-physics-research.php "Simon's Rock"), Oscar obtained a _Bachelor of Arts_ in computer science from Bard College at Simon's Rock and a _Master of Science_ in mathematics from the [University of Alaska Fairbanks](https://sites.google.com/alaska.edu/oihernandez "oihernandez@alaska.edu").

His experience lies primarily in security, information technology, governance and the public interest.
