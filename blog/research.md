This week, I'm learning about connections and holonomy.

Previously, I studied liberal arts/sciences and researched computer science, machine learning, and category theory.
Now, I'm interested in topology, geometry, gauge fields, knots, gravity, and their applications to finance, economics, philosophy, and the humanities.

A picture is composed of two parts: the figure and the ground.
In physics, the ground is played by general relativistic spacetime and the figure is played by quantum field theoretic interactions of forces and matter.

Of course, the distinction between figure and ground is not always clear, such as in the physical problem of quantum gravity whose popular proposed solutions include string theory and loop quantum gravity.
