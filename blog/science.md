## Strategy 
His main interests lie in physics, mathematics, philosophy, and engineering. 
Since taking [Category Theory Seminar](http://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/qg-fall2018/ "Category Theory and the Bar Construction"), Combinatorial Theory, and Network Theory Seminar with [John Baez](http://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/ "John Baez") at UC Riverside, he now follows the [Azimuth Forum](http://www.azimuthproject.org/azimuth/show/Applied%20Category%20Theory%20Seminar "Applied Category Theory in the Azimuth Forum").
In general, he is interested in applications of category theory to programming language theory (operational semantics), networks, control systems, physics (quantum gravity), homotopy type theory, databases, and machine learning.

He currently approaches engineering from the theoretical and applied extremes by reading about [network theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.05625 "A Compositional Framework for Passive Linear networks") and [ROS](https://wiki.ros.org/ "Robot Operating System"). 
He plans on steadily filling the gaps by learning traditional electrical engineering and gaining hands-on experience with mechanical engineering design.

## Execution
He works as a computer engineer, freelances as a software engineer, and researches artificial intelligence.
