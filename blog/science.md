# Sciences 

Oscar has a wide range of interests throughout the sciences, arts, and humanities, although he is chiefly interested in the formal, natural, applied, and social sciences behind technology and startups.

## Strategy 
His main interests lie in physics, computer science, philosophy and foundations of mathematics.
As such, he recently participated in a [Category Theory Seminar](http://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/qg-fall2018/ "Category Theory and the Bar Construction") at UC Riverside taught by [John Baez](http://math.ucr.edu/home/baez/ "John Baez"), and looks forward to following the upcoming [Applied Category Theory Seminar](https://johncarlosbaez.wordpress.com/2018/12/14/applied-category-theory-seminar/ "Applied Category Theory Seminar") on the [Azimuth
Forum](https://forum.azimuthproject.org/discussion/2359/applied-category-theory-seminar "Applied Category Theory in the Azimuth Forum").
In general, he is interested in applications of category theory to programming language theory (operational semantics), networks, control systems, physics (quantum gravity), homotopy type theory and univalent foundations.

He currently approaches engineering from the theoretical and applied extremes by reading about [network theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.05625 "A Compositional Framework for Passive Linear networks") and [ROS](https://wiki.ros.org/ "Robot Operating System"). 
He plans on steadily filling the gaps by learning traditional electrical engineering and gaining hands-on experience with mechanical engineering design.

## Execution
In order to stay sane and make progress somewhere, he also dedicates time to a few [e-courses](/portfolio/courses) including [Social Psychology](https://www.coursera.org/learn/social-psychology "Social Psychology") and [Galois Theory](https://www.coursera.org/learn/galois/ "Introduction to Galois Theory").
