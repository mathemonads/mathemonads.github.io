Oscar grew up in the East San Francisco Bay Area, fond of TV, music, pop non-fiction, and science. At the LPS Hayward high school, he tutored his peers in English, Chemistry, and Mathematics, dipped his feet in the Science Bowl team, and initiated the "fencing" (playing with foam swords) and "debate" (vague pseudointellectual conversation) teams. Advised by Ms. Sandy Little, he applied to a small liberal arts college in Western Massachusetts.

Upon admission to Bard College at Simon's Rock at the age of 14, he enrolled in all kinds of classes including Latin, Nature & Literature, Drawing, and Formal Logic before receiving an associate's degree, therafter concentrating in mathematics and computer science. He tutored 7 college subjects, 4 high school classes, held several student leadership positions, and held jobs in IT and game development, attained several scholarly research publications, wrote a few more
manuscripts, and learned a buttload of things that haven't seen the light of day.
He was mainly interested in economics, physics, functional programming, type theory, mathematical logic, and philosophy.

Upon graduation, he became a 5th Grade 12:1:1 Humanities teacher at a public charter middle school in Harlem, where he taught World History, English, and Computer Science. After the first semester, he became a bum and decided to join a startup for free and freelance as a software engineer to pay the bills. On the side, he kept pursuing his interests by reading and taking e-courses on physics, mathematics, engineering, and philosophy.
