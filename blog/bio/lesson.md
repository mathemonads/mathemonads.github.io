c = lambda v, c = speed of light

planck: energy is quantized, not continuous

E = h v, v = frequency of photon

Metal has electrons bound to positive charges in the nuclei.
A photon with enough energy can knock an electrons loose, now called a photoelectron

E_photon = E_0 + KE_photoelectron
KE = E_photon - E_0 = work function = minimum energy needed to free the electron
