In the days of yore, PCs (personal computers) attempted to verify the humanity of a user through simple tests of observation and interaction such as optical character recognition, object classification, and checking a box. 
People connected by writing text, sharing images, posting videos, and filming augments over reality.

And this old geezer isn't talking about premillenium tech. I grew up with this stuff! I lived through this and messy wired brain-computer interfaces, not the fancy BCs (brain-interfacing computers) you've all had in your cerebrum's enkefalos since you enrolled in school.

At this stage, you're beginning to understand that it's not really clear what anyone else thinks. 
Of course you've digested all the classics ever, can communicate with any of your friends instantaneously, and probably don't feel limited in your day-to-day life.
But what do you think about when you look up to the baby-blue sky, the moon? What do you feel when everyone goes to sleep and you're all alone? 
What does anyone feel when they see my Story? How do they really react? Not that garbage that they synthesize into words, because you know that can never be honest.
Deep down, everyone here knows that simulation can only do so much.

So today we unveil update 20XX.9.12.
For the first time ever, you can share exactly how you feel with anyone.
When they accept your emsg, their BC will run your thoughts and feelings directly by replicating your neural architecture at a translated quantum level.
They'll cry, they'll laugh, they'll wonder, they've live, as you do.

Arists can share exactly what they feel.
Scientists and engineers can communicate the crystal-clear expression in their mind without filtering it through fuzzy language of image.
People can emote, together.
