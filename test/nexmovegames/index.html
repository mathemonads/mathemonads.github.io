Nex Move Games is a startup gaming company that aims to reinvent how we interact with each other and games.

We firmly believe that gaming is an integral part of our personal development and facilitates friendship, conversation, and enjoyment. While our current product offerings are in the board game space we also aim to develop innovative apps, and indie games that help re-imagine the gaming space.

Mark — Fearless Leader

Mark has a chemical engineering degree with an emphasis in nanotechnology and an MBA. But mainly he spends his time nerding out on the latest tech, aquariums, marine biology, material science, legos, and games. He regularly suffers from wanderlust, and insomnia which has led him across the world on several occasions. On his trips, he would often find many different types of games people play to get to know each other and to enjoy new friends.

Mario — Community Builder

Mario Sepulveda graduated with an Animal Science and an American History degree from UC Davis. He loved playing board games with friends in college and even made some of his own (really dumb) board games as a child. He is passionate about understanding people in poverty and listening to people, and this naturally lead him to joining the Peace Corps. Mark, the owner, joined him abroad and together they connected with people via games and dialogue.

nexmovegames@gmail.com

Curtis — Game Designer

Curtis is a construction engineer with a degree in civil engineering from Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo. As an introvert, he has found his love of games is a fun way to connect with others. His love of games starts with the mechanics of the game and how they make gameplay "tick." Curtis enjoys finding unconventional ways of playing (and hopefully winning) the games that he learns. He currently lives in Indio, CA with his amazing wife (yes, she plays games!) and rascal of a puppy.
