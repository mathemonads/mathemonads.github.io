# Sciences 

Oscar has a wide range of interests throughout the sciences, arts, and humanities, although he is chiefly interested in the formal, natural, applied, and social sciences behind technology and startups.

## Math and Physics 

Having studied a wide breadth of mathematics and computer science, he is especially interested in the philosophy and foundations of mathematics. As such, he participated in a Category Theory Seminar at UC Riverside taught by John Baez, and looks forward to following the upcoming Applied Category Theory Seminar on the Azimuth Forum.
In general, he is interested in applications of category theory to homotopy type theory, physics, networks, and control systems.
Having taken the introductory physics sequences on Khan Academy and edX, he looks forward to learning more about categorical physics and quantum gravity.   

## Engineering
He approches engineering from both the theoretical and applied extremes. Mainly, he reads about network theory from a categorical perspective, and ROS from a software engineering perspective. He plans on slowly filling the gaps by learning traditional electrical engineering and gaining hands-on experience with mechanical engineering design.
